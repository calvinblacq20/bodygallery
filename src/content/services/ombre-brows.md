Ombré brows (sometimes called powder brows) use fine pigment to create a soft, powdered look: lighter at the front and fuller towards the tail, a bit like brow make-up. We map the shape with you first, so you approve how your brows will look before we start.

It's semi-permanent. The colour fades gradually, so most people book a top-up to keep their brows fresh.
