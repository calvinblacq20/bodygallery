Microneedling uses a pen with very fine needles to make tiny, controlled channels in the skin. As the skin repairs itself, it can help soften the look and texture of stretch marks over a course of sessions.

Every plan starts with a consultation. We look at your stretch marks, ask about your skin and health history, and agree the plan and full cost before your first session. Results vary from person to person.
