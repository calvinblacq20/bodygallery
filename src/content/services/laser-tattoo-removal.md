Laser tattoo removal breaks tattoo ink into tiny particles that your body clears away over the following weeks. Tattoos fade gradually over a course of sessions rather than disappearing in one visit.

At your consultation we look at the tattoo's size, colours and age, check your skin, and give you an honest view of what to expect.
