Fibroblast treatment uses a plasma device to make tiny, controlled points on the skin's surface, which encourages the skin to tighten and renew as it heals.

Plasma treatments carry a higher risk of dark marks (hyperpigmentation) on deeper skin tones. Your consultation checks whether it's right for your skin, and we may suggest a patch test first. Results vary from person to person.
